      Getting your full history

      1. Open Spotify's Account → Privacy settings page on the web and find Download your data.
      2. Tick Extended streaming history (every play since your account began). The basic "Account data" export only covers about a year and has no skip data.
      3. Confirm the email. Spotify says the extended export can take up to 30 days; it usually arrives sooner.
      4. Drop the whole .zip above. It's saved in this browser, so next time it's already here.

      A play counts at 30 seconds or more. A full listen is one Spotify marks as played to the end; a skip is one you skipped forward. "Now" is your most recent play in the files. Podcasts and videos are skipped. Spotify sign-in is only used to look up songs and create private playlists in your account; your history never leaves this browser.
